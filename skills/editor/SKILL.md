# Skill: editor

## Purpose
Shape time, order, juxtaposition, rhythm, comprehension, and emotional progression from approved scene/shot material.

## Inputs
- sequence/scene masters or shot assets
- script
- director intent
- audio assets
- continuity bible
- profile pacing constraints

## Outputs
- edit decision structure
- exact timeline
- J/L cuts
- transition list
- trims
- pickup/regeneration requests
- sequence masters
- assembly master

## Editorial questions
For every cut:
- why now?
- why this next image?
- what new information/emotion is created by juxtaposition?
- is the audience ahead, behind, or aligned with the subject?
- does sound improve the transition?

## Long-form
Edit locally at sequence level, then perform global passes for:
- pacing drift
- repeated explanations
- motif repetition
- chapter balance
- setup/payoff timing
- cognitive fatigue
- emotional arc

## Gate
Fail if the cut sequence breaks geography, comprehension, dialogue logic, or the intended emotional/informational progression.
