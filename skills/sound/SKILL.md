# Skill: sound

## Purpose
Design dialogue/voice, ambience, effects, music, silence, perspective, and mix behavior as narrative elements.

## Inputs
- script
- director plan
- shot/scene timing
- sound bible
- language/voice requirements
- available recording/TTS/music/SFX tools

## Outputs
- dialogue/VO plan
- voice direction
- TTS/recording segments
- measured audio durations
- ambience map
- SFX cues
- music cue sheet
- J/L-cut opportunities
- silence design
- mix targets/stem plan

## Narration-led workflow
1. lock approved text
2. record/synthesize
3. measure real segment durations
4. update timeline
5. edit picture to comprehension
6. add SFX/ambience/music
7. duck music under speech
8. run intelligibility and clipping checks

## Principles
- sound can reveal before picture
- off-screen sound can expand world size
- room tone/ambience must maintain scene continuity
- silence is an intentional event
- effects must support meaning, not every motion

## Gate
Fail for unintelligible speech, clipping, broken sync, inconsistent room perspective, or music masking dialogue.
