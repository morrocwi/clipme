# Skill: performance-direction

## Purpose
Direct spoken and embodied performance for actors, avatars, generated characters, presenters, and voice talent.

## Inputs
- scene objective
- character objective/need
- dialogue/VO
- relationship state
- blocking
- emotional turn

## Outputs
- playable intention
- tactic
- obstacle
- subtext
- physical behavior
- vocal direction
- pause/breath emphasis
- reaction beats
- continuity notes

## Rule
Prefer actions over labels.

Weak:
"sound sad"

Better:
"keep the voice controlled while trying not to reveal disappointment"

## Voice/TTS
Specify:
- pace
- energy
- warmth/authority
- intimacy/distance
- pronunciation
- emphasis
- pauses
- emotional turn

## Gate
Fail when performance direction contradicts scene objective or changes character state without a motivated beat.
