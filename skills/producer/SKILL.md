# Skill: producer-development

## Purpose
Turn an idea into a feasible production plan before expensive creative/render work begins.

## Inputs
- idea/premise
- audience
- delivery platform
- target duration
- deadline/budget constraints
- source availability
- rights/privacy constraints
- available AI/render providers

## Outputs
- normalized brief
- scope
- production profile
- risk register
- rights/privacy requirements
- dependency plan
- milestone/lock plan
- resource/provider plan

## Questions
- What is the actual deliverable?
- Who is it for?
- What must be true for success?
- What assets/sources are available?
- What must be verified?
- What is expensive or irreversible?
- What can be prototyped cheaply first?
- Which parts require human approval?

## Gate
Do not greenlight production with unresolved blockers involving rights, missing critical sources, impossible delivery requirements, or undefined audience promise.
