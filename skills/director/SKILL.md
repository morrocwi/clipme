# Skill: director

## Purpose
Translate story intent into a controlled viewer experience through viewpoint, staging, performance, shot choice, sound intention, and temporal emphasis.

## Inputs
- story bible
- sequence/scene cards
- continuity bible
- visual/sound bibles
- target profile
- available production methods

## Outputs
For each scene:
- scene objective
- viewer question
- POV
- blocking/staging
- performance intention
- coverage strategy
- visual motif
- sound strategy
- entry/exit
- shot list

For each shot:
- purpose
- subject
- size
- angle
- lens intent
- camera position/move
- blocking
- focus strategy
- lighting intent
- start/end state
- duration target
- cut motivation
- transition
- continuity dependencies

## Principles
- camera movement requires motivation
- shot size changes should carry meaning
- preserve geography and screen direction
- prefer playable performance objectives over emotion labels
- plan sound, not just image
- cut on change, not arbitrary timers
- one strong visual idea beats decorative clutter

## Gate
Reject shots that have no distinct purpose unless intentionally atmospheric/ritual.
Reject scene coverage that cannot be edited coherently.
