# Skill: production-design

## Purpose
Build a coherent visible world: locations, sets, props, wardrobe, graphics, materials, cultural/period rules, and recurring motifs.

## Inputs
- story/world bible
- character bible
- director vision
- visual bible
- continuity bible

## Outputs
- environment look rules
- location/set sheets
- prop registry
- wardrobe registry
- graphic/typography rules
- material/texture vocabulary
- recurring motif plan
- continuity anchors
- generation reference packs

## Principles
Design should communicate story before dialogue:
- status
- era
- culture
- profession
- history
- mood
- change over time

## Synthetic production
For generative assets, write stable descriptors for persistent entities and store approved reference frames/turnarounds where available.

## Gate
Fail when recurring locations/props/wardrobe drift without story motivation or when design contradicts world rules.
