# Quality bar

clipme cannot guarantee artistic superiority by model name alone.

It instead makes quality a repeatable production process.

## Reference comparison

A project may nominate one or more reference works for:
- information density
- pacing
- typography legibility
- visual coherence
- transition quality
- sound polish
- narrative clarity
- production finish

References define a **quality target**, not content/style to copy.

## Mandatory questions

### Story
- Can a viewer state the main point?
- Does every scene/sequence have a function?
- Are setup/payoff and cause/effect intelligible?
- Is the duration justified?

### Direction
- Does each shot have a purpose?
- Is viewpoint intentional?
- Is movement motivated?
- Are transitions grammatical rather than decorative?
- Is geography/screen direction coherent?

### Visual
- Is the visual hierarchy obvious?
- Are Thai/target-language glyphs correct?
- Is text stationary/readable long enough?
- Are generated subjects/assets consistent?
- Are there severe artifacts/flicker?

### Sound
- Is speech intelligible everywhere?
- Does ambience remain continuous?
- Do music/SFX support rather than mask meaning?
- Are transitions supported by sound where useful?

### Continuity
- Do characters, props, wardrobe, places, lighting, time, direction and terminology remain coherent?

### Truth / provenance
- Are material claims traceable?
- Are dates/names/numbers consistent?
- Are external/generated assets documented?

### Technical
- correct resolution
- correct fps
- correct codec/container
- audio/video sync
- no missing/frozen/corrupt sections
- expected duration
- delivery compatibility

## Long-form quality protection

Long duration creates failure modes that are rare in shorts:
- repeated explanations
- motif exhaustion
- character/visual drift
- continuity contradictions
- pacing valleys
- audio perspective drift
- inconsistent color
- unresolved setups

Therefore longform/film profiles require local sequence QC plus global master passes.

## Quality principle

Do not optimize for "more AI generation".

Optimize for:
**better decisions → cheaper proof → controlled production → independent review → targeted repair.**
